# RIVER TOWNS
## The Pagan's Motorcycle Club in Delaware County, Pennsylvania

**Format:** Documentary short in three acts, Ken Burns treatment
**Target runtime:** ~22 minutes
**Narration:** Single narrator (warm, unhurried, mid-register)
**Visual grammar:** Slow pans and push-ins on archival stills (the "Ken Burns effect"), letterboxed 2.39:1 crops of 16:9 frames, dissolves of 1.5–2 seconds, black-and-white and sepia archival material intercut with present-day color drone and street photography. Generated title/chapter/map cards for this film are in `images/`.

> **Editorial note.** This film is journalism about a real organization and real, in some
> cases living, people. Every factual claim in the narration is keyed to the source list in
> `SOURCES.md`. Allegations are attributed ("prosecutors said," "according to court
> records"), acquittals and dismissals are stated on screen wherever charges are mentioned,
> and no current private address is identified. Interview subjects are placeholders to be
> cast; their lines here are *indicative content*, not scripted quotes.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Pacing:** average shot length 6–8 seconds in Acts I–II, tightening to 4–5 seconds in Act III; let the coda breathe past 10.
- **Moves:** never zoom and pan simultaneously on faces; push-ins reserved for turning points (the patch, the evidence table, the courthouse doors).
- **Color:** archival material stays in its native tone (no colorization); present-day footage graded warm to match the paper palette of the cards.
- **Music:** two themes only — the piano/cello main theme and a percussive variant for Act II; silence is the third instrument.
- **Legal review:** all named-individual passages and lower thirds must clear counsel before lock; see `SOURCES.md` for the claim-by-claim key.
