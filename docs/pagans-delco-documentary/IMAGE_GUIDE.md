# Image Guide

Two kinds of visuals serve this film: the **generated cards** in `images/` (finished,
ready to cut in), and **archival photographs** to be licensed (this file tells you what to
pull and how to move the camera over it).

## Generated cards (`images/`)

All cards are 1920×1080 PNG, designed to the "Rust Archive" philosophy
(`DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md`) — aged paper, letterpress serifs, oxblood accents. Regenerate
with the seed-stable script used in production if edits are needed.

| File | Use | Suggested move |
|------|-----|----------------|

| `02-act1-card.png` | Act I chapter card, 1959–1979 | static, 4 s, 1.5 s dissolves |
| `03-act2-card.png` | Act II chapter card, 1980–1999 | static, 4 s |
| `04-act3-card.png` | Act III chapter card, 2000–present | static, 4 s |

| `06-timeline.png` | Chronology plate (Act III open) | slow left-to-right pan along the baseline, 10–12 s |

## Archival photographs to source

Ken Burns move grammar used below: **push** (slow zoom-in, ends on detail), **pull**
(zoom-out, reveals context), **pan** (lateral drift), all 4–15 s, eased in/out, never
pan + zoom on a face.

## Licensing and ethics notes

- Photographs of identifiable living club members require counsel review even when
  licensed; prefer backs, silhouettes, hands, and machines over faces.
- DOJ/DEA/ATF press images are public domain; wire-service images are not.
- No image may identify a current private residence or active clubhouse address.
