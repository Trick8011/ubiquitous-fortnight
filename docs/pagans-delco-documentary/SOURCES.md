## Club history

- Founded 1959 by Lou Dobkin in Prince George's County, Maryland; turn to one-percenter
  status mid-1960s; rapid early-1970s growth under John "Satan" Marron; ties to La Cosa
  Nostra families; one of the "Big Four" outlaw motorcycle clubs.
  — [Wikipedia: Pagan's Motorcycle Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pagan%27s_Motorcycle_Club),
  [SlashGear history](https://www.slashgear.com/1970180/pagans-motorcycle-club-biker-gang-history/)
- Surtur patch, no bottom rocker, East Coast territory with Pennsylvania at its heart.
  — [One Percenter Bikers: Pagans MC](https://onepercenterbikers.com/pagans-mc/)
- National headquarters / Mother Club placed in Delaware County, Pennsylvania by
  club-tracking sources. **Verify before broadcast** (attribute to law-enforcement /
  gang-intelligence reporting, not stated as fact).
  — [One Percenter Bikers: Pagans MC](https://onepercenterbikers.com/pagans-mc/)

## Philadelphia region, 1970s–1990s

- Pagans and Warlocks as the dominant Delaware Valley clubs from the 1970s.
  — [Wikipedia: Warlocks Motorcycle Club (Pennsylvania)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warlocks_Motorcycle_Club_(Pennsylvania))
- 1980s Philadelphia meth trade; Jimmy DeGregorio ("Jimmy D") and "Pagan Purple";
  Scarfo-era street-tax war with the Philadelphia mob — kidnappings and assaults; club
  refusal to pay.
  — [Billy Penn: how the Pagans are connected to Philadelphia](https://billypenn.com/2015/08/10/pill-mills-an-train-honeys-how-the-pagans-motorcycle-gang-is-connected-to-philadelphia/),
  [Ranker: Pagans facts](https://www.ranker.com/list/pagans-motorcycle-gang-facts/christina-chilin)

## Delaware County, present day

- June 28, 2021 federal case (D.N.J.): eleven members/associates charged — narcotics,
  firearms, violent crimes in aid of racketeering; wiretaps and undercover officers;
  10 firearms and 800+ grams of methamphetamine seized. Includes Glen "Glenny" Turner,
  73, of **Marcus Hook, PA**, longtime member — ~450 g of methamphetamine recovered in a
  Dec 10, 2020 search of his residence, per the charging announcement. Charges are
  allegations; confirm individual dispositions before broadcast.
  — [DEA press release](https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2021/06/28/pagans-motorcycle-gang),
  [DOJ D.N.J. press release](https://www.justice.gov/usao-nj/pr/eleven-members-pagan-s-motorcycle-club-charged-narcotics-distribution-firearms-offenses),
  [ATF press release](https://www.atf.gov/news/press-releases/eleven-members-pagan%E2%80%99s-motorcycle-club-charged-narcotics-distribution-firearms-offenses-and)
- March 3, 2024, ~3:30 p.m.: shooting outside the Wawa at 390 E. Chester Pike, **Ridley
  Township**; eight men involved, all current or former Pagans per police; one man shot in
  the leg; nine arrests reported. Joseph Krider, 48, of **Drexel Hill**, charged with
  attempted murder and related counts; the attempted-murder count was later dismissed and
  a jury acquitted him of most remaining charges.
  — [6abc](https://6abc.com/post/ridley-township-pa-shooting-wawa-pagans-motorcycle-club-delaware-county/14493944/),
  [NBC10](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/news/local/wawa-shooting-pagans-outlaw-motorcycle-gang-arrested/4295152/),
  [CBS Philadelphia](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/9-pagan-motorcycle-gang-wawa-shooting),
  [Patch: verdict](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/haverford/verdict-reached-pagans-related-shooting-delco-wawa)
- "Blue Wave" national expansion from 2018 under national president Keith "Conan" Richter
  (Long Island chapter); chapters in 49 states within a decade; Pennsylvania described as a
  long-time influence center; leadership later passed to Robert "Big Bob" Francis.
  — [Gangster Report](https://gangsterreport.com/gr-exclusive-pagans-mc-los-malos-leaders-break-bread-at-n-j-diner-in-wake-of-cletus-ayers-boss-promotion/),
